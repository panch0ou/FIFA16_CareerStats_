FIFA 16 CAREER STATS
Windows x64 · 2015/16 Career Mode

1. Extract the entire ZIP into a folder.
2. Open CareerStats.exe.
3. If needed, choose your FIFA 16 game folder and profile folder.
4. Click Launch FIFA 16.

Keep Data and Runtime beside CareerStats.exe. The two screens are prepared
automatically the first time you launch. Edited roster databases can work with
the supported FIFA 16 PC executable, save metadata, and screen templates.
Historical coverage ends before 2015/16 and depends on the included FIFA IDs.
FIP, modern seasons, and other executables remain outside the verified scope.

If your FIFA profile is missing, open FIFA normally once and close it.
After an interrupted session, close FIFA and choose Recover. Use View Logs
if the launcher reports a problem. Local settings and tracking are stored in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\FIFA16CareerStats.

See Notices for credits, data coverage, and bundled runtime notices.
