# Credits and redistribution inventory

FIFA 16 Career Stats builds on the 12Final history project and the read-only save reader from the companion FIFA 16 TransferEngine project. The source archive includes the reader and the local APT library. The APT library's Cargo metadata declares MIT. The original code has no newly assigned project-wide license. MIT remains the proposed license for code owned by the project. [MIT license text](https://opensource.org/license/mit)

Both archives include the preserved historical player and club inputs. The player history draws on Salimt's Transfermarkt-derived dataset, the project archives labeled Kaggle2 and FIFA Model, and earlier and manually sourced entries. Club records retain their citations and coverage notes. The archive names alone do not establish upstream license terms. See [DATA.md](DATA.md) for coverage and provenance. [Salimt football-datasets](https://github.com/salimt/football-datasets)

The user archive includes the portable observer and screen helpers, Python with Tcl/Tk, and the PyInstaller bootloader. Python's bundled license and the available Rust, Cargo dependency, and PyInstaller notices are under `Notices`. Dependency versions are pinned in the Cargo and Python build files. [PyInstaller license](https://pyinstaller.org/en/stable/license.html)

FIFA 16 executables, the FIFA database, archives, game DLLs and extracted or regenerated game screens are outside both ZIPs. The app generates its two screens locally from the user's verified game files. FIFA 16 and its assets belong to their respective rights holders. The archives also exclude personal saves, registry data, settings, logs, and earlier lab binaries.

These archives were prepared locally for the project owner. A package inventory and its credits do not grant permissions from third-party rights holders.
